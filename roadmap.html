What Claude needs from you

  1. Sign in to CKN on the laptop (and tap Yes once on Psychiatry Online) and say so. Claude then works the sign-in routes one paper at a time while the session lasts, which is about a day.
  2. PDFs from your phone: the Wiley, SAGE, Elsevier and Taylor & Francis papers on the full-text list. Their bot checks block Claude's browser but not yours. Attach them here, ten at a time.
  3. Later: calibration round 2 (about 30 minutes) and a review of Claude's readings, strongest cases first. Both on your phone.

Done means: every case coded in the full schema, from the full text wherever it can be had and from the abstract, labelled, where it can't; every case with a reading; the screening rules passing calibration (kappa 0.7 or more); and a site that reads well to someone who has never heard of the project.
